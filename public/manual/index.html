Welcome to ReactiVision! Here’s how to use the application.

User Information

Before you begin, it's important to provide some information about yourself. This data is used to provide more accurate and contextualized analysis of your reaction times.

  • Age and Gender: Your age and gender can affect reaction times.
  • Wears Glasses: This helps in understanding visual acuity.
  • Visual Fatigue: A rating of your current eye strain.

Calibration

Calibration is a crucial step to ensure the accuracy of your reaction time measurements. It establishes a baseline by measuring your device's latency.

  • Start Calibration: Go to the "Calibration" tab and click the "Start" button.
  • Follow Instructions: The screen will guide you through a series of clicks. Click as soon as the screen turns green.
  • Complete: After a few attempts, the calibration will be complete, and you can proceed to the test.

The Reaction Test

The main test measures your reaction time. You'll need to complete 5 valid attempts.

  • Start the test: Go to the "Test" tab and click "Start."
  • Wait for Green: The screen will turn green after a random delay. Click as fast as you can.
  • Faults: Clicking too early will result in a fault.
  • Progress: A progress bar will show your progress toward the 5 valid attempts.

Results & AI Analysis

This section displays your test history and provides AI-powered analysis.

  • View Results: Your average reaction time, number of faults, and other data are displayed in a table.
  • Filtering and Pagination: You can filter the results by different criteria and navigate through the pages.
  • Graph Visualization: A graph at the top of the section visualizes your reaction time trends.
  • AI Analysis: Click the "AI" button to get a detailed analysis of a specific test.
  • General Analysis: A collapsible section at the top provides a general analysis of all your results.

Tech Stack

ReactiVision is built with a modern, robust technology stack:

  • Frontend: Next.js (App Router), React, TypeScript
  • Styling: Tailwind CSS with Shadcn/UI
  • State Management: Zustand and React Hooks
  • Backend: Supabase (PostgreSQL)
  • AI: Google Gemini via Genkit
  • Charts: Recharts

Project Structure

The codebase is organized into the following main directories:

  • /src/app - Main application pages
  • /src/components - Reusable React components
  • /src/hooks - Custom hooks for shared logic
  • /src/lib - Core logic and Supabase client
  • /src/ai - AI-related flows
