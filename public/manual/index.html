Welcome to ReactiVision! Here’s how to use the application.

The Reaction Test

The main goal is to measure your reaction time. The test consists of 5 attempts.

  • Fill in your data: Provide your age, gender, glasses status, and visual fatigue level.
  • Start the test: Click the large box when it says "Click to start".
  • Wait for green: When it turns green, click as fast as possible.
  • Invalid clicks: Clicking early counts as a fault.
  • Review: After 5 attempts, your average time and faults are saved.

Calibration

Calibration establishes your baseline for more accurate reaction results.

  • How it works: You react to a sound cue to isolate cognitive delay.
  • Perform the test: Focus on the audio signal, not visual cues.
  • Result: The average time becomes your calibration value.

Results & Export

This section shows all your completed test results.

  • Average Time: The mean of your valid reaction times.
  • Calibrated Average: Average adjusted using calibration data.
  • Export to CSV: Download your data for analysis.
  • AI Analysis: Use AI tools to gain insights from your data.

Tips for Accurate Results

  • Stay focused and minimize distractions.
  • Perform calibration before main tests.
  • Keep your hand comfortable on your input device.
  • Provide accurate demographic data for context.
